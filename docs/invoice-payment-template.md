# DZOFT — Invoice payment details and payment-link QR

## Purpose
DZOFT has its own numbered invoice and private PDF archiving workflows. The new output uses a clean DZOFT-branded presentation with the payment guidance visible in the Revolut reference screenshot, **without claiming to be Revolut or issuing a second Revolut invoice**.

### What appears
- Agreed project and invoice numbers, service, parties, SIRET, issue date, amounts and relevant tax wording are still sourced from the accepted quote and issued DZOFT invoice.
- Only when **the invoice has a positive remaining balance**: payment beneficiary, IBAN, BIC, bank name and payment reference (official invoice number) sourced from **Admin → Settings → Payment methods**.
- If Settings includes a valid HTTPS card-payment link: a local, offline-generated QR code plus a clickable link in the print-preview; a vector QR code and readable link are included in newly generated PDF archives where space permits.
- If payment has already fully cleared: **Facture réglée**, without a payment QR or request to pay again.

The QR stores only the configured **payment page URL**. It does **not** create a Revolut payment request, fix the transaction amount, transfer the invoice number to the payment provider, or confirm settlement. Clients must verify the amount and reference before paying. The payment backend still requires explicit verified receipt confirmation.

### Single official numbering system
Use **one official invoicing system** for each transaction. If an official invoice was already issued by Revolut or another provider, attach its existing PDF to DZOFT; do not use DZOFT to issue a duplicate numbered invoice for the same sale. DZOFT's own F-YYYY-NNNN sequence is separate only when deliberately selected as the issuing system.

### Existing invoices
Previously issued PDFs are immutable and are **not** silently edited or regenerated by this change. Newly issued and newly archived DZOFT documents pick up payment settings only during PDF generation. Never add a QR to a paid invoice already in the client portal.

### Technical safeguards
- Vendored MIT-licensed `qrcode-generator` JavaScript with its original copyright/license, loaded locally from the DZOFT website, no external QR service, no remote invoice/image requests.
- Only HTTPS checkout URLs of reasonable length are accepted; invalid or missing URLs omit the QR.
- Bank details are taken from the owner-maintained settings, never from sample screenshots or guesswork.
- The owner-only PDF generator does not send emails, modify project receipts or change invoice numbering.

### Legal and quality review
Before issuing, review mandatory French invoice fields and tax treatment. The existing VAT-free wording applies only when the actual business qualifies for `TVA non applicable, article 293 B du CGI`. A printable PDF is not automatically a structured e-invoice for the French electronic invoicing reform. Review cross-border invoices, payment terms, date of service, late-payment penalties and buyer identifiers with an accountant or qualified advisor.
