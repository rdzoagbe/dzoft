Frequently asked questions

What people often ask me

How long does it take?

A one-page website: a few days. A showcase website: one to two weeks. An app: four to eight weeks depending on the features. The timeline is written in the quote.

Why is it cheaper than an agency?

I am neither an agency nor a career developer. I design and build with modern tools, including artificial intelligence, and rely on more than six years of corporate IT experience to deliver something reliable. I work alone, with no overheads: you pay for the work, not for the organisation around it.

Who owns the website and the app?

You do. The domain name, hosting and Apple and Google developer accounts are in your name. If you ever change provider, nothing breaks. On delivery, I give you the handover sheet (in French) listing all your accounts.

Do I have to pay the app stores?

Yes, if you want an app on the stores: Apple charges $99 per year and Google a one-off $25, billed directly to your account. An installable web app (PWA) avoids these fees.

What happens after launch?

The monthly plan covers hosting, updates and small changes. It is optional: you can also manage your site yourself, and I will show you how.
