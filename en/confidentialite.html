2. Data collected

  • Quote request: first name, business, email or phone, type of project and platform, budget, ticked needs and project description.
  • Appointments: if you book a call, your first name, last name, email and phone, and the slot chosen.
  • Client area: email address and password. The password is not stored as such, but in hashed form (irreversibly transformed): I cannot read it.
  • Project follow-up: your messages, the files you send (logo, photos, texts), your step approvals with the name entered, the date and the time.
  • Acceptance of a quote: the name entered, the date and time, the IP address, the email address that received the signature code, the version of the terms and conditions accepted and your choice about starting before the end of the withdrawal period. The six-digit code is never stored in plain text, only in hashed form (with a random salt) from which it cannot be recovered.
  • Reviews: if you leave a review, the rating, the text, the name and business you choose to display, and whether or not you agree to its publication.
  • Invoicing: name, company, address, amounts and invoice references.
  • Email notifications: an internal log keeps the date, type, recipient and delivery status of important notifications for 24 months (quote sent or accepted, messages, step approval, milestone, invoice, received file or review). The log does not copy message text or file contents, but a notification email may include a message or the name of a received file. An official invoice PDF is attached only when DZOFT selects that option; other documents are never attached automatically.

4. Retention periods

  • Request not followed up: no more than three years after collection, or after the last contact you initiated.
  • Client: during the project and any support, then three years after our last exchange.
  • Invoices and accounting records: ten years from the end of the financial year. Accepted quotes and proof of acceptance: generally five years for commercial contracts; an electronically concluded consumer contract worth €120 or more is kept for ten years from delivery or performance where that rule applies.
  • Quote requests that do not become a project/client: the copy in the DZOFT dashboard is kept for up to three years after collection; deletion is currently checked manually as part of retention reviews. The Formspree copy follows the account plan and settings separately; email copies follow the recipient inbox settings.
  • Notification log: 24 months after the event. Email copies received by the client or DZOFT follow their inbox settings and Brevo’s settings.
  • Published reviews: until you ask for them to be removed.
  • Signature codes: usable for ten minutes. The code itself is never stored; its salted hash, masked destination, attempts and timestamps remain linked to the quote until the quote is permanently deleted.

You can ask to close your client area and erase your data. I also check files and copies held by service providers; accounting or contractual records that must be kept remain for the applicable period.

6. Security

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Each client only sees their own requests, quotes, messages and files.
  • Files are not public: they open with temporary links.
  • My administration access is protected by two-factor authentication.

7. Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies and uses no audience measurement or advertising tools. When you sign in to your client area, your browser keeps your session in local storage. If you use a private link instead of an account, the browser also stores that link token: anyone who has the link can access the corresponding client area. Keep it confidential. When you create an account, the old private link is revoked and you use your account from then on. Closing the area or signing out clears the session data from that device.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict its use, to object to it, or to receive it in a reusable format. Write to roland.dzoagbe@hotmail.com: I reply within one month at most.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).
