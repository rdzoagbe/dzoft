The need

Web platform · SaaS

StackLens

The need

Companies pay for forgotten or duplicate software, or for tools whose price went up without anyone noticing. Accountants see it in the bank statements, with no tool to deal with it.

What I built

  • A software subscription audit based on bank statements and French accounting files (FEC): duplicates, forgotten subscriptions, price increases, renewals.
  • A free audit to try it in a few minutes.
  • A workspace for accounting firms and part-time CFOs, with up to fifty client files.
  • Tracking of tools, staff, devices, access, leavers and security.
  • Paid subscriptions with Stripe; data stored in the European Union, some processing in the United States (see the list of sub-processors).
